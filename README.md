# Consumer Complaint Trends & Response Intelligence Platform

### AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab Internship | Week 4

## Project Overview

This project focused on analysing consumer complaint records from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) relating to Bank of America, covering the period 2017–2023.

The objective was to investigate complaint trends, understand the major sources of customer complaints, and examine response timelines to identify areas that may require operational attention.

The project involved transforming raw complaint data into an interactive Power BI dashboard to support data-driven understanding of consumer complaints and response performance.

## Business Problem

Financial institutions receive consumer complaints across different products and services. Without proper analysis, it can be difficult to understand:

- How complaint volumes change over time.
- Which financial products generate more complaints.
- What issues contribute to customer dissatisfaction.
- How complaints move through the response process.
- Where response delays may require further attention.

The central objective was to turn complaint records into meaningful insights that could help stakeholders understand customer friction points and response patterns.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Power BI:** Dashboard development, data visualisation, and reporting.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- **DAX:** KPI creation and analytical calculations.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Business Problem Definition

Established the business questions guiding the investigation, with emphasis on complaint trends, product categories, and response timelines.

### 2. Data Preparation with Power Query

Imported and transformed the CFPB complaint dataset, preparing the relevant fields for analysis and visualisation.

### 3. Data Modelling and KPI Development

Organised the data for reporting and used DAX to create calculated measures and performance indicators required for the analysis.

### 4. Dashboard Development with Power BI

Developed a four-page interactive dashboard designed to provide different perspectives on consumer complaint patterns and response intelligence.

## Areas of Analysis

The dashboard explored:

- Complaint volume trends over time.
- Complaint distribution across financial products.
- Major complaint categories and issue patterns.
- Complaint forwarding and response timelines.
- Potential response bottlenecks.

## Key Learning Outcomes

Working on this project strengthened my practical understanding of:

- Data preparation and transformation using Power Query.
- Developing analytical measures with DAX.
- Building multi-page interactive dashboards in Power BI.
- Analysing time-based trends and categorical distributions.
- Translating customer complaint data into business-focused insights.
- Connecting analytical findings to operational questions.

## Project Deliverable

An interactive, four-page Power BI dashboard presenting consumer complaint trends, product-related complaint patterns, and response timeline analysis.

## Conclusion

This project reinforced the importance of looking beyond complaint volumes to understand the patterns behind customer dissatisfaction and the processes involved in responding to complaints.

It also strengthened my ability to approach analytics from a business perspective, starting with the problem, preparing the data, developing relevant measures, and communicating findings through visualisation.
